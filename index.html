01 — About

A student chasing the physics behind the software

AS

Robotics & AI · 2025–2029

I'm an undergraduate in Robotics and Artificial Intelligence at VNR Vignana Jyothi Institute of Engineering and Technology, Hyderabad. I like problems that sit where computation meets the physical world — a control loop, a sensor budget, a link margin, or the amount of signal a satellite can afford to lose.

I'm in two of VNRVJIET's student clubs: AIRBOTS, the robotics club, where I work on robots end to end — mechanisms, sensors and the control software that ties them together — and Kakşyā Śāstra, the space club, where students build and fly real hardware: water rockets, CanSats and balloon payloads. Alongside that I write review papers on space and defence technologies, from satellite jamming and anti-jamming to stealth engineering and quantum communication.
